[[Read the creators' reflection->reflection]] [[Play again and challenge your fate->start]] //Passage 20 • Reflection & Bibliography// ##Behind the Crossroads ###Group Reflection We designed this story as a mission-based journey because career decisions rarely happen in a straight line. The three career doors create nonlinear pathways, while shared scenes reconnect the narrative and show that students in different fields still face pressure, rejection, uncertainty, and ethical choices. Early decisions return later: résumé honesty affects the background check, preparation affects the interview, and relationships affect the final test. Career, Integrity, and Wellness scores demonstrate that "success" has several meanings. Humor makes the story inviting, while countdowns, mysterious messages, and delayed results create pressure and engagement. ###Team Contributions Person 1: Passages 1–6, opening mystery, identity pressure, résumé dilemma, career selection. Person 2: Passages 7–14, career missions, waiting game, interview, rejection, secret offer. Person 3: Passages 15–20, consequences, final crisis, endings, technical testing, reflection. ###Starter Bibliography Vanderbilt University Career Center. Career exploration and preparation resources. Twine Reference and Documentation. Group-created narrative, dialogue, scoring system, and original ethical dilemmas.
