# Media placeholders

Keep the black, gold, and white visual system when adding media.

- Put photographs or illustrations in `assets/images/`.
- Put music and sound effects in `assets/audio/`.
- Compress images before uploading. Prefer `.webp` or `.jpg`.
- Prefer `.mp3` or `.ogg` audio files under 5 MB.
- Record the creator, source URL, license, and access date for every asset in `MEDIA_CREDITS.md`.
- Never autoplay long music. Give the player a visible play or mute control.

Suggested filenames:

- `campus-night.webp`
- `career-vault.webp`
- `courtroom.webp`
- `corporate-tower.webp`
- `research-lab.webp`
- `graduation.webp`
- `ambient-theme.mp3`
- `notification.mp3`
