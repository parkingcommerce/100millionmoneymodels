# Twenty-passage production plan

| Passage | Owner | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Commodore Crossroads | Person 1 | Establish the 30-day countdown and mystery |
| 2. The Pressure Call | Person 1 | Introduce family and social pressure |
| 3. The Secret Invitation | Person 1 | Begin the Career Vault mission |
| 4. The Résumé Test | Person 1 | Create the first ethical decision |
| 5. The Career Vault | Person 1 | Build anticipation before choosing |
| 6. Choose Your Mission | Person 1 | Branch into three career routes |
| 7. Courtroom Mission | Person 2 | Law-specific ethical dilemma |
| 8. Corporate Tower | Person 2 | Finance/consulting tradeoff |
| 9. Locked Laboratory | Person 2 | Medicine/research authorship dilemma |
| 10. Application Submitted | Person 2 | Suspense and delayed result |
| 11. Waiting Room | Person 2 | Side mission that changes interview performance |
| 12. Interview Battle | Person 2 | Conditional choices based on preparation |
| 13. The Fall | Person 2 | Rejection and cliffhanger |
| 14. The Devil's Shortcut | Person 2 | Alumni favor dilemma |
| 15. Background Check | Person 3 | Bring the résumé decision back |
| 16. Two Offers | Person 3 | Prestige-versus-purpose decision |
| 17. The Emergency | Person 3 | Family responsibility under time pressure |
| 18. The Betrayal Test | Person 3 | Final ethical dilemma involving a friend |
| 19. Graduation Day | Person 3 | Calculate and reveal multiple endings |
| 20. Reflection | Person 3 | Explain design choices and list sources |

## Required testing routes

1. Honest résumé → Law → Prepare → Reject shortcut → Purpose offer.
2. Exaggerated résumé → Finance → Party → Accept shortcut → Prestige offer.
3. Fake résumé → Research → Prepare → Background-check failure → Founder route.

The team should verify that the opening decisions visibly affect Passage 15 or Passage 19.
